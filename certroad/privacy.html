Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

In short

  • CertRoad has no accounts, no ads and no tracking.
  • Your answers, scores and progress are stored only on your device.
  • We do not collect, sell or share personal data.

Who we are

CertRoad is made by Amber Road, a sole proprietorship registered in Finland (Business ID 1840478-3). Amber Road is the controller for the limited information described below. Contact: amberroadapps@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

To show your progress, CertRoad saves your practice history, scores, answered and missed questions, and which exams you have unlocked. This data is stored locally on your phone (iPhone or Android) in the app's own storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time in the app (About → Reset progress) or by deleting the app.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) on iPhone and by Google (Google Play) on Android. We never see your name, payment card, Apple ID or Google account. The store tells the app only which products you have bought, so the right exams can be unlocked and restored. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

To confirm purchases and restore them on a new device, CertRoad uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID and your purchase receipts from Apple or Google. It does not receive your name or contact details. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Contacting us

If you email us (for example to report an error in a question), we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We keep the conversation only as long as needed to handle your request and then delete it.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or health data.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties for marketing.

Children

CertRoad is a study tool for professional certification exams and is not directed at children. Because the app does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data about you, and to object to its processing. Because CertRoad keeps your data on your device, you can delete it yourself at any time. For questions about an email conversation with us, contact amberroadapps@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
