Support

CertRoad helps you prepare for Microsoft certification exams with practice questions, explanations and timed mock exams. Need help? Email us at amberroadapps@gmail.com – we usually reply within a few days.

Found an error in a question?

Tap the flag on the question, or email us the exam code and the question text. Every report is checked against Microsoft Learn and fixed in the next update.

Frequently asked questions

What is free?

Every exam has 45 free practice questions. A practice round picks 20 of them at random. Unlocking an exam gives you all its questions, timed mock exams and the weak-spots drill.

Is it a subscription?

No. You can unlock one exam with a one-time purchase, or buy All exams once – it includes every exam available now and every exam added later.

How do I restore my purchases on a new phone?

Open an exam's unlock screen and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID (iPhone) or Google account (Android) you used for the original purchase. Purchases do not move between iPhone and Android.

How do I reset my progress?

Go to About → Reset progress. This deletes your history and stats on this device. Your purchases are kept.

Does CertRoad collect my data?

No. There is no account and no tracking, and your progress stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

Are these real exam questions?

No. All questions are written independently to match the published skills outline of each exam. They are practice questions, not exam dumps. Study with the official Microsoft Learn material as well.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by the store you bought from. On iPhone, request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, request it in Google Play – see Google Play refunds.

Can you add another exam?

Yes, suggestions are welcome – email us which exam you are studying for.
